🌿 CirclePlan

Your privacy matters to us.

Updated September 27, 2026. CirclePlan is owned by Aureon and Static Labs.

What we use

We use your sign-in email, display name, and the calendar details you choose to add to save plans, synchronize circles, send invitations, and provide reminders. Plans may include dates, notes, participants, comments, and responses. Settings and calendar copies are also stored on your device.

Sharing and contacts

You choose who receives an invitation when you share by text, WhatsApp, email, or another app. CirclePlan uses your phone’s sharing menu and does not read or upload your address book. Circle members can see plans according to their access. Owners can revoke circle access. Single-plan invitations are limited to the invited sign-in email and expire after seven days. A recipient’s separate copy cannot be recalled.

Our service providers

Google supports Google sign-in and Google Play subscriptions. Supabase hosts authentication and shared calendar data. These providers process information needed to operate CirclePlan. We do not sell calendar content or show advertising. Google Play handles payment details; CirclePlan receives purchase verification and expiry details, not your card number.

Reminders and your choices

Notifications are optional. Home-screen widgets and lock-screen reminders may display plan titles. You can edit or delete plans you manage, revoke circle membership, and manage or cancel Plus in Google Play. Signing out does not delete cloud records. Uninstalling clears local data but not cloud data or copies another person received.

Account and data deletion

We will verify that the request belongs to you before deleting the account and records we control. Recipient copies and provider backups may remain under their retention rules.
